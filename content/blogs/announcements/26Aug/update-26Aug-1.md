Thank you for your continued use and support of Open Endfield Map.

Over the past few weeks, we have continued refining the everyday OEM experience. This update includes **push notifications**, new **keyboard shortcuts**, and several **localization improvements**. Meanwhile, the next industrial content update is now in preparation, and we are also evaluating the possibility of bringing a **route planner** to OEM.

## Notifications

OEM now includes on-site notifications. You can receive notifications when other community members reply to your comments, as well as updates on the review and publication status of your contributions, making it easier to receive feedback and learn from the community.

![Figure 1: Push Notification Interface](/blogs/announcements/26Aug/fig1.webp)

## Shortcut Updates

Several shortcuts have been added, including **Command/Ctrl + K** to quickly activate search input and **Command/Ctrl + H** to toggle UI visibility. You can view all currently available key hints in **Preferences**. Future features will continue to follow the same control patterns, making zooming, switching views, and other common map actions easier to learn.

![Figure 2: Available Shortcuts](/blogs/announcements/26Aug/fig2.webp)

## L10N Improvements

We have continued filling gaps in localized interface text, standardizing terminology across map, marker, and account features, correcting inflection and long-text layout issues in several languages, and adapting date and time formats to the preferences of each country and region. These improvements also cover recently introduced community and notification features, providing a more consistent experience across languages.

Localization remains an ongoing effort. If you notice a translation error, truncated text, or content displayed in the wrong language, please contact us through one of the channels below and include the page location and a screenshot when possible.

![Figure 3: Date Formats in Several Languages](/blogs/announcements/26Aug/fig3.webp)

## Update Preview

The next batch of map content will focus on the game's **industrial systems**. We are organizing the relevant data, categories, and presentation, and will add the content to the map in an appropriate form once it has been verified.

We are also exploring a possible **route planner** that could help players organize clearer exploration or gathering routes across multiple objectives and plan in-game zipline layouts. This feature is still in the evaluation and prototype stage. Its final form, scope, and release schedule have not been decided, and whether it enters full development will depend on its practical value, data quality, and development progress.

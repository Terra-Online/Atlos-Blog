title: "服务中断公告"
description: "账户登录、云同步与图片上传服务暂时中断"
date: 2026-07-17
---

We sincerely apologize for the current service outage. The issue was caused by a failure in Cloudflare's D1 infrastructure. We are actively working with Cloudflare Support to resolve the incident and discuss possible compensation.

Your local collection progress is not affected. Once the service is restored, all locally saved data will be synchronized back to the cloud automatically at the earliest opportunity.

Previously uploaded images remain accessible through our pre-warmed KV cache. However, signing in, cloud synchronization, and uploading new images are temporarily unavailable until the database service has fully recovered.

Thank you for your patience and understanding. We will provide further updates as soon as more information becomes available.

---

对于当前的服务中断，我们深表歉意。本次问题由 Cloudflare 的 D1 基础设施故障所致。我们正积极与 Cloudflare 支持团队合作解决此次事故，并就可能的补偿方案进行沟通。

您的本地收集进度不受影响。服务恢复后，所有保存在本地的数据将尽快自动同步回云端。

此前上传的图片仍可通过我们预热的 KV 缓存正常访问。但是，在数据库服务完全恢复之前，账户登录、云同步和上传新图片功能将暂时无法使用。

感谢您的耐心与理解。一旦获得更多信息，我们将尽快发布后续更新。

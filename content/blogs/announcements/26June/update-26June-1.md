Thank you for using and supporting OEM.

During the previous release cycle, Open Endfield Map officially launched its **Account System**, along with several closely related features including **real-time locator sync, screenshot uploads, official map import, and OEM Cloud**.

OEM now have **more than 1,200** registered users, and screenshots contributed by pioneer users now **cover more than 2,700 collectible locations across Talos-II**. We strongly recommend creating an OEM account to securely sync your progress across devices.

In Version 1.3, OEM will introduce more community features including **marker comments, multi-image uploads, marker collection statistics, and personal contribution dashboards**, while also adding support for **Turkish** and **Ukrainian**. Our goal is to make OEM the preferred mapping tool for Endfield players worldwide, delivering **timely and efficient data** alongside an **elegant UI/UX**, without relying on laggy alternatives or suffering from outdated and inaccurate data.

If you find OEM helpful, we would greatly appreciate you **recommending it to fellow Endfield players**. The following sections provide details about recent updates and may help you make better use of OEM's features.

## Marker Updates

### Layer Badges

As shown below, all markers located on B/L layers can now display their own layer information. Clicking the marker allows you to switch directly to the appropriate layer, making navigation in complex indoor environments significantly easier.

![Figure 1: Marker Layer Overview](/blogs/announcements/26June/fig1.webp)

### Marker Updates

The following marker categories have recently been added or are currently planned:

1. All markers in **Hidden Blade Valley**;
2. Renewable **Wooden Boxes** for players interested in completion tracking;
3. Manually maintained **NPC Shop** locations (planned);
4. Manually maintained locations of fragments for **Locked Crates** (planned).

If you would like to help maintain marker data, feel free to join our [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/BFMAKZSUG7).

## Feature Updates

As shown below, we have recently enabled OEM Cloud. As a core OEM account feature, cloud sync is **enabled by default after registration and continuously synchronizes local data automatically**. It helps maintain data consistency across multiple devices and browsers.

In most cases, existing OEM data from different devices can be merged when needed. You may also import your existing progress directly from the official map.

Thank you for your continued support and use of OEM.

Before the next major game update, we are introducing the second batch of features following the launch of the OEM account system: **Marker Screenshot Upload and the Karma System**.

For markers that require collection, you can now follow the guide below to upload third-person in-game screenshots, enhancing the experience for you and other users:

## How to Upload Screenshots

Currently, you can click on map markers under the **Collection, Exploration, and Archives** categories to see an image slot in the detail modal. If the slot is empty, simply click it to upload an image.

![Fig 1: Image Upload Area](/blogs/announcements/26May/fig4.webp)

Images you upload will be displayed after passing moderation, so there may be a slight delay.

## User-Generated Content Guidelines Summary

To maintain an effective and accurate data environment, please be sure to read and adhere to the following guidelines before uploading. For full details, please refer to the project's [UGC Content Statement](/docs/ugc) and [Community Guidelines](/docs/community-guidelines).

**You may upload:**
* Clear **third-person screenshots** that showcase the marker's position in game.
* Photos taken using the in-game camera that contain only necessary game visuals, free of redundant visual clutter.
* Moderately annotated auxiliary guide images that help identify complex terrain or hidden items.

**You may NOT upload:**
* Screenshots containing sensitive personal information (such as real names, contact details, game UIDs, plaintext passwords, etc.).
* Images that infringe upon third-party intellectual property rights or are unrelated to in-game collection.
* Images containing politically sensitive, pornographic, violent, or gory content.
* Images with spam advertisements, promotional QR codes, or cheat software watermarks.

**Privacy and Moderation Notice:**
* **Visibility Risk**: Once your screenshots pass automatic or manual review, they will become **publicly visible within OEM**. All users accessing this project will be able to see them. Please review your images carefully before uploading and perform any necessary cropping to absolutely avoid privacy leaks.
* **Security Moderation and Actions**: Uploaded content may be subject to security and compliance reviews. Developers reserve the right to directly hide or delete violating images without prior notice; for users who maliciously abuse the upload interface or repeatedly violate the rules, developers reserve the right to penalize their accounts.

## Karma and User Groups Summary

We have officially activated the Karma system. Karma is a way to measure a user's level of activity on Open Endfield Map. We will also assign Pioneer or Admin roles to users who actively contribute, ensuring a smoother contribution experience.

For detailed information regarding Karma and the user group system, you can refer to the [Guide for Karma and User Groups](/community/karma).

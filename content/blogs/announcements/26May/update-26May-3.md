---
title: "CN Mirror Maintenance"
description: "The CN mirror of Open Endfield Map will undergo maintenance starting May 26. Please back up your data in advance"
date: 2026-05-25
---

Thank you for your continued support and use of Open Endfield Map over the past months.

We need to update the ICP registration information for our mainland China deployment. **During the registration migration period, the CN mirror domain (opendfieldmap.cn) may become temporarily unavailable or fail to resolve properly**. Therefore, we plan to temporarily suspend services for the CN mirror **starting from 21:00 on May 26, 2026 (UTC+8)**.

The maintenance and registration migration process is expected to take approximately 5 to 12 business days. We will make every effort to restore access to the CN mirror before the release of version 1.3.

During the suspension period, the global site (opendfieldmap.org) will remain fully operational.

If you need to continue using the map service during this period, we recommend exporting your data in advance using the export function shown below and transferring it to our [Global Site (opendfieldmap.org)](https://opendfieldmap.org). We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding.

![Fig1: Export and import operations](/blogs/announcements/26May/fig5.webp)
*Figure 1: Exporting and importing data through the "Protocol & Storage" panel in the top headbar*

You can click the corresponding icon in the website’s top navigation bar to open the "Protocol & Storage" panel, then click the "Export Data" button to export your OEM progress locally. Afterwards, you may import the data at the same location on the international site (opendfieldmap.org)￼ to complete the migration process.”

Thank you for your continued use and support of Open Endfield Map. Marker data for the Version 1.6 update, **Sanctuary of Ink**, is now available. We have also made the following improvements to the community experience.

## User Center

![](/blogs/announcements/26Oct/fig1.webp)

You can now open the [User Center](https://oem.re/me) from the account panel in the sidebar to view your account details and community contributions in one place.

- Change your avatar on the **Personal Information** page; your selection syncs immediately.
- View your community images and comment history in the **Content Center**, and follow each submission from receipt through automated and manual review to publication or archiving.
- Select the marker linked to a contribution to return to its location on the map.

We plan to keep improving the User Center throughout this version, with more features such as reviewing past progress and linking multiple account channels. Stay tuned!

## Rich-Text Comments

![](/blogs/announcements/26Oct/fig2.webp)

Marker comments now support more ways to express yourself. You can use bold, italics, underlining, strikethrough, and inline code, as well as add safe links and **emoji**!

## A Localized Community

OEM's UI translations are now connected to [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/oem). We welcome polyglots and native speakers to help review translations so more Endministrators around the world can use the map with ease. Thank you sincerely for your time and contributions.

Open Endfield Map is a community-driven, long-term maintained open-source non-profit project. The project is primarily maintained by serveral developers using spare time; the work covers **map data organization and updates**, **UI/UX design**, **multilingual I18N**, **frontend/backend development**, UGC moderation, and many other areas such as tooling, automation pipelines, and infrastructure operations.

Over the past four months, Open Endfield Map's frontend services have processed over **500M HTTPS requests**, consuming a cumulative **4.5+ TB** of bandwidth.

Backend services handle a monthly average of **3B+ bytes** of response traffic, with monthly Container CPU time and Worker CPU time exceeding **30M milliseconds**; KV/DO reads and writes both exceed **100K Pairs**; we also maintain a D1 database with monthly average reads/writes exceeding **200M rows** and a Redis middleware layer processing a monthly average of **over 2M commands**, serving **80,000+ monthly active users** - and these numbers are still growing.

Since development began in February 2025, Open Endfield Map has consistently upheld the principles of **open source, openness, and non-profit operation**. We will **never introduce ads**, and will **never use the map site to generate traffic benefits** for ourselves. All support goes directly toward the project's continued operation and development.

If you enjoy this project, find it helpful, or believe in our development and design philosophy and want to see it maintained stably for the long term, you can support us through the following channels.

## About Supporter Rewards

We've always wanted to ensure that support and sponsorship **never compromise** the openness of Open Endfield Map itself. Whether or not you choose to support the project, all users will continue to enjoy full, free access to the core map features. We will never sell anything that affects fairness of functionality, nor will we make map data, marker features, basic service settings, or an ad-free experience into paid content.

With that as our foundation, we'd rather offer supporters rewards with sentimental or symbolic value, rather than additional functional privileges. From development logs and design drafts to exclusive appearance themes and physical keepsakes, these are meant to represent recognition of our development philosophy, design process, and community culture — not special access.

Your support helps this project keep existing, and gives more users a better tool experience and more stable maintenance, which in turn fosters a positive feedback loop within the game's content and community. We sincerely thank every supporter for their contribution and recognition.

## In Closing

Open Endfield Map started as a personal passion project and has gradually grown into an open-source map platform serving tens of thousands of players across multiple language communities worldwide. Along the way, we've received countless pieces of feedback, suggestions, translation contributions, bug reports, and code submissions from around the globe. For an open-source project, this kind of spontaneous help from the community matters far more than any infrastructure or tech stack ever could.

Whether it's sponsoring the project, submitting an issue, contributing translations, contributing code, helping with testing, or simply using the site and recommending it to friends — you're already supporting Open Endfield Map, and supporting OEM, in your own way.

Thank you to everyone willing to help Open Endfield Map keep moving forward.

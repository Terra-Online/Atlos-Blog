Thank you for your continued support and use of OEM.

Since our last update announcement, the OEM development team has shifted its focus to backend development, striving to bring you an even better user experience through a robust, reliable, and flexible backend architecture. Today, we are absolutely thrilled to bring you a major feature update focusing on the **User System**. Reading the introduction below might just level up your experience, so let's dive in:

## User Registration
The user card is located at the bottom of the sidebar. You can click the avatar to register, sign in, or manage your profile (**Figure 1**).

![Figure 1: User Panel Overview](/blogs/announcements/26April/fig1.webp)

At this current stage, you can use your email to receive a verification code, or use supported third-party OAuth providers (currently Discord and Google) to register and sign in to your Open Endfield Map account (**Figure 2**).

![Figure 2: Registration Panel Overview](/blogs/announcements/26April/fig2.webp)

You will need to:
- First, enter a valid email address to receive and input the verification code;
- Set a password consisting of 8-20 alphanumeric characters (which must include at least one uppercase letter).
Only then can the registration form be successfully submitted. Once everything checks out, we will automatically submit it for you. Alternatively, you can simply press the Enter/Return key to submit the form!

*During our testing phase, we received feedback regarding **Outlook's** restrictions on new sender domains. Currently, emails sent to **@outlook.com** addresses have a chance of ending up in the spam folder—please be sure to check both your inbox and your spam folder after requesting a verification code!*

## Profile Overview
Your profile contains the following information (**Figure 3**):
![Figure 3: Profile Overview](/blogs/announcements/26April/fig3.webp)
* **Your Group and Karma.** Unauthenticated users will appear as a "Guest"; registered users will default to the "Normal" group. Karma is the battery-like bar beneath your group badge, representing your activity and contribution levels. Additional groups (like "Pioneer" and "Admin") and detailed Karma rules will tie into upcoming community features.
* **Account Registration Date (Since).**
* **Your Nickname.** You will be prompted to set up a nickname right after your first registration—and you can change it anytime!
* **Your UID.** Your UID is assigned when you first set up your nickname and cannot be changed. Don't worry, though! This UID is purely for the display panel when sharing your profile on social media and has no direct ties to your core identity or account data.
* —And of course, cute avatars of our admins and operators!

## A Sneak Peek at the Community System
Our original plan was to complete all backend milestones before doing a full deployment. We have mapped out a ton of account-related features including, but not limited to:
* **Cloud sync** for collection progress—seamlessly share your collection progress across any device or browser;
* **Marker screenshot viewing & uploading**—view in-game screenshots uploaded by other active users to aid your exploration. Anyone can upload screenshots and share the collective fruits of our labor.
* The Karma — Karma reflects your activity. You can boost it by using OEM, uploading markers, and providing valuable comments;
* In-game progress synchronization;
* Community profile card sharing;
* ...and more!

However, due to the massive development workload and accompanying operational costs, we couldn't squeeze all of this into the expected dev cycle. Therefore, we decided to roll out the user registration system first and invite everyone to try it out!

Registered users will be the first to receive notifications when new features drop, allowing you to safely and quickly manage your game progress.

If you are as excited about these features as we are, stay tuned to OEM! You can also lend the dev team a hand via the **[Support Us]** channel at the bottom left of the page. Every bit of support goes directly toward accelerating the development of these awesome new features!

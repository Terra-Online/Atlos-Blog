These guidelines supplement the [UGC Statement](./ugc) and apply to point images, comments, replies, votes, reports, and other community interactions.

## Principles

- Treat others respectfully and share objective, useful information related to map exploration.
- Do not mislead players, pollute point data, or manipulate community interactions.
- Follow applicable law and do not abuse uploads, comments, translation, reports, votes, or API features.
- Do not disclose passwords, tokens, contact details, or other sensitive information.

## Content standards

| Type | Allowed | Not allowed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Images | Clear screenshots, routes, and explanatory annotations for a game point | Personal data, irrelevant images, cheat advertising, malicious QR codes, sexual, violent, hateful, extremist, or politically sensitive material, and misleading point data |
| Comments and descriptions | Point explanations, route advice, constructive corrections, and relevant discussion | Abuse, harassment, discrimination, cheats, spam, phishing, malicious code, fraud, or another person's private data |
| Points and data | Verified coordinates, routes, and map annotations | Knowingly wrong, fabricated, duplicate, meaningless, or automated data, including bulk injection through an API |
| Votes and reports | Good-faith quality feedback and reports of specific violations | Multi-account manipulation, retaliatory or false reports, mass harassment, or automated influence on ranking or review |

## Submission, review, and publication

Images and comments first undergo automated text and image review. Material that does not pass automatically may receive human review. Approved content is public; material under review, awaiting manual review, or withdrawn is normally not visible to ordinary visitors. Edited comments are reviewed again, and an existing public version or edit snapshot may remain until the new version passes.

Public comments may receive pre-generated or on-demand machine translations. Translations can be inaccurate and are not the author's original wording. Reports trigger status changes or review, but their number is not proof of a violation.

## Enforcement

We may hide, reject, flag, withdraw, or remove content and restrict accounts for serious or repeated violations. UGC is submitted independently by users, so verify images, comments, and routes against the game. Character binding and live location through Skland or SKPORT also carry risks from third-party API changes, expired authorization, and game-service controls; see the [Disclaimer](./disclaimer).

* **Reports and compliance**: [report@opendfieldmap.org](mailto:report@opendfieldmap.org)

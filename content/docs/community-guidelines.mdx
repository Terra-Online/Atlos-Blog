This document serves as an effective supplement to the Project's [UGC Content Statement](./ugc), aiming to further clarify the community behavioral standards and content guidelines you must adhere to while using Open Endfield Map (hereinafter referred to as "OEM" or "this Project").

Unless otherwise specified, the definitions and interpretations of terms in this document shall be consistent with those in the [UGC Content Statement](./ugc).

## Community Environment and Behavioral Principles

**Open Endfield Map** is committed to building an open, friendly, accurate, and efficient game map community. To safeguard the user experience for everyone, you must adhere to the following basic principles when using this Project's services:

- Respect other users and community members; keep content objective, accurate, and game-related.
- **DON'T BE A DICK!** Do not deliberately sabotage map data, mislead other players, or disrupt community order.
- Comply with the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China and your local jurisdiction; do not abuse platform APIs, upload capabilities, or synchronization features; do not disseminate illegal, harassing, advertising, or malicious content.

## Upload Content and Usage Norms

In addition to the prohibited content explicitly listed in the UGC Content Statement, the following rules further apply to map screenshots, comments, markers, and related interactive content.

| Content Type | Permitted Content | Prohibited Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screenshots & Images** | <ul><li>Third-person screenshots clearly displaying game locations, scenes, or routes.</li><li>Images serving to explain map mechanics, terrain structures, or hidden elements.</li><li>Moderately cropped, compressed, or annotated auxiliary guide images.</li></ul> | <ul><li>Images containing sensitive personal information (e.g., real names, contact info, account passwords, chat logs).</li><li>Meaningless images or images unrelated to *Arknights: Endfield*.</li><li>Images bearing cheat tool promotions, watermark advertisements, malicious QR codes, or traffic-diverting content.</li><li>Images involving pornography, violence, gore, terrorism, hate speech, or politically sensitive topics.</li><li>Screenshots that clearly mislead players, forge coordinates, or maliciously pollute map data.</li></ul> |
| **Comments, Text & Descriptions** | <ul><li>Objective location descriptions and route suggestions.</li><li>Constructive feedback and correction suggestions.</li><li>Experience sharing and community communication related to map exploration.</li></ul> | <ul><li>Insults, defamation, personal attacks, or malicious harassment.</li><li>Discriminatory, hateful, or extremist speech.</li><li>Dissemination of cheat tools, illicit means, private servers, or content disrupting game fairness.</li><li>Spam, malicious flooding, or repetitive meaningless posts.</li><li>Malicious code, scripts, phishing information, or account scam content.</li><li>Text content involving the unauthorized disclosure of others' private data or account information.</li></ul> |
| **Markers & Data** | <ul><li>Actual verified in-game locations and coordinates.</li><li>Reasonable farming routes, exploration paths, and map annotations.</li></ul> | <ul><li>Obviously incorrect, maliciously forged, or misleading map information.</li><li>Large amounts of repetitive, meaningless, or automatically generated data.</li><li>Abusing APIs to inject spam markers or anomalous data in bulk.</li></ul> |

## Privacy Protection and Moderation Mechanisms

1. **Public Visibility Risk Warning**: Once your uploaded third-person screenshots, comments, and coordinate markers are submitted and approved, you authorize us to display them publicly. **All users accessing this Project will be able to view them.** Please review carefully before uploading, and **absolutely avoid including or leaking your personal privacy data in screenshots or comments**.
2. **Automated and Manual Moderation Compliance**: To ensure a safe community environment, public text content (such as comments) and screenshots you submit may be temporarily transmitted to third-party automated moderation APIs (e.g., **OpenAI Moderation API**) for security and compliance screening. This screening is strictly for intercepting violating information; such data **will never** be used for AI model training.

## Violation Handling and Disclaimer

1. **Disposal Rights**: The developer team reserves the right to **unilaterally modify, hide, or permanently delete any content violating these guidelines without prior notice**. For users with severe or repeated violations, we will directly freeze, ban, or permanently delete the user's account and all associated data.
2. **Game Decision Disclaimer**: All UGC is independently generated and provided by users; this Project serves solely as an information display platform. We offer no guarantees regarding the absolute accuracy of UGC. This Project and its developer team bear no responsibility for any in-game decision-making errors or any joint losses incurred due to a user relying on erroneous UGC provided by other users (e.g., false markers, misleading routes).
3. **Official Risk Disclaimer**: This Project is an unofficial community initiative. If third-party authorization binding (such as Skland or SKPORT) is involved, there is an inherent risk of interception by official firewalls. This Project bears no joint liability for any game account risk control or restrictions resulting from a user's personal actions.

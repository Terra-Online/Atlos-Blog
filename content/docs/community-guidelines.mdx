These guidelines supplement the [UGC Statement](./ugc) and apply to current or future point images, comments, replies, point and route contributions, votes, reports, and other community interactions.

## Principles

- Treat other users with respect and provide objective, relevant, and useful information about map exploration.
- Do not deliberately mislead players, pollute point content, or manipulate community interactions.
- Follow applicable law and do not abuse upload, comment, translation, reporting, voting, or API capabilities.
- Protect your privacy and that of others. Do not disclose passwords, tokens, contact details, or other sensitive information.

## Content standards

| Content type | Allowed | Prohibited |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screenshots and images** | <ul><li>Clear third-person screenshots showing game points, scenes, or routes</li><li>Images that explain map mechanics, terrain, or hidden content</li><li>Guide images with reasonable cropping, compression, or annotations</li></ul> | <ul><li>Images containing real names, contact details, account passwords, chat logs, or other sensitive personal information</li><li>Irrelevant or meaningless images unrelated to Arknights: Endfield</li><li>Cheat advertising, promotional watermarks, malicious QR codes, or traffic-diversion content</li><li>Sexual, violent, graphic, terrorist, hateful, or politically sensitive content</li><li>Images that clearly mislead players, fabricate points, or maliciously pollute map data</li></ul> |
| **Comments, text, and descriptions** | <ul><li>Objective point descriptions and route advice</li><li>Constructive corrections</li><li>Relevant experience and community discussion about map exploration</li></ul> | <ul><li>Insults, defamation, personal attacks, or malicious harassment</li><li>Discriminatory, hateful, or extremist statements</li><li>Promotion of cheats, unfair play, or unauthorized game servers</li><li>Spam, flooding, or repetitive low-value content</li><li>Malicious code, scripts, phishing, or account fraud</li><li>Unauthorized disclosure of another person's private or account information</li></ul> |
| **Points and data** | <ul><li>Game points and coordinates verified in play</li><li>Reasonable collection routes, exploration paths, and map annotations</li></ul> | <ul><li>Clearly incorrect, fabricated, or misleading map information</li><li>Large volumes of duplicate, meaningless, or automatically generated data</li><li>Bulk insertion of unlawful points or abnormal data through an interface</li></ul> |
| **Votes and reports** | Genuine quality feedback and good-faith reports of specific violations | Multi-account manipulation, retaliatory reporting, mass harassment, false reports, or automated interference with ranking and review |

## Submission, review, and publication

1. New images and comments first undergo automated review, and edited comments are reviewed again. The system reviews relevant text and images separately. Material that does not pass automatically proceeds to human review.
2. Approved content is published. Content under review, awaiting human review, or withdrawn is normally not visible to ordinary visitors.
3. Edited comments require a new review. The system may retain the existing public version or an edit snapshot until the new version is approved.
4. Public comments may receive pre-generated or on-demand machine translations. Machine translations can be inaccurate and should not be treated as the author's original wording.
5. A report can change content status or initiate another review. The number of reports is not itself proof of a violation; moderators assess the content and context.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may hide, reject, flag, withdraw, or remove content and may restrict community features for accounts that commit serious or repeated violations. Submitters may withdraw content when its status allows or request removal of public content.

UGC is submitted independently by users, and the project does not guarantee absolute accuracy. Verify images, comments, and route advice against the game.

Skland or SKPORT character binding and live location can also be affected by third-party API changes, expired authorization, and controls imposed by the game service. See the [Disclaimer](./disclaimer).

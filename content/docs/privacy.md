This policy covers data processed when you browse the map, register, synchronize progress, submit community content, or bind a game character. Client interaction state is local-first, but map resources, public UGC, sign-in, and API requests still pass through our servers and infrastructure providers. See the [Personal Information Collection and Use List](./data-collection) for field-level detail.

## Information we process

We process account information, OAuth identity and public profile data, character-binding data, and submitted UGC that you provide. Service use can also produce completed-point IDs and sync metadata, IP addresses and technical logs, current coordinates while a binding is active, and votes, reports, and review status. Theme, language, filters, map view, and similar UI preferences normally remain in your browser.

## Uses and third parties

We use data to operate accounts, synchronize progress, display location, moderate and display UGC, protect the service, apply rate limits, and improve features. Cloudflare provides network, computing, and storage; Upstash supports limits and short caches; OpenAI moderates text and images; Google Cloud Translation translates approved comments; Google, Discord, and GitHub provide chosen sign-in; and Resend sends necessary account email. These providers may process data in other regions under their own policies.

## Storage, security, and retention

Accounts, sessions, progress, bindings, and structured UGC records are stored in Cloudflare D1; images are stored in R2. Binding tokens are AES-GCM encrypted, passwords are one-way hashes, and transfers use HTTPS/TLS. KV, Durable Objects, Cache API, and Upstash support coordination, caching, and limits. We retain data for the account, binding, or content lifetime, or as needed for security, disputes, backups, and legal duties. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

## Cookies, disclosure, and your rights

Cookies and local storage support sign-in, security, preferences, and caching; see the [Cookie Policy](./cookie). Approved images, comments, public author information, and interaction statistics are visible to visitors. You can request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, and withdraw or delete UGC where the product permits. Clearing browser data does not automatically delete server data.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, infrastructure, or law change. Material changes are published on the site or sent to the associated email.

* **Privacy and support**: [privacy@opendfieldmap.org](mailto:privacy@opendfieldmap.org)

Open Endfield Map allows signed-in users to submit images and comments for existing map points and to participate in replies, votes, reports, and content maintenance. This statement supplements the [Terms of Use](./tos), [Privacy Policy](./privacy), and [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines).

## Scope of UGC

**User-generated content (UGC)** means the following content and interaction records that you actively submit or create through the project's public community functions:

* Real-world or guide images associated with existing map points;
* Point comments, replies, and comment edits;
* Votes, reports, withdrawals, or removal requests relating to public images and comments;
* Shareable routes, point annotations, or other map contributions when the project provides the corresponding feature;
* Corrections and suggestions submitted through official feedback channels.

Completed-point status, theme, filters, map view, and similar UI preferences are not public UGC.

## Rights and license

1. **Your rights**: You retain rights that you lawfully hold in original content and represent that you have the right to submit the relevant images and text.
2. **Service license**: By submitting UGC, you grant the project a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license, for as long as needed to provide and maintain the service, to store, copy, process, display, and distribute the content for operation, publication, moderation, translation, caching, format conversion, backup, and service improvement.
3. **Public display**: Approved public images, comments, public author information, and interaction statistics can be viewed by all visitors. Other users may quote or retain public content.
4. **Effect of deletion**: After withdrawal or deletion, we stop public display according to the applicable process. Related records may be retained for a necessary period to resolve disputes, support security audits and backups, or fulfill legal duties.

## Content requirements

You must not submit:

1. Content that infringes copyright, trademark, portrait, privacy, or other third-party rights;
2. Unlawful, sexual, hateful, harassing, threatening, extremist, or severely violent content;
3. Cheats, unfair-play tools, unauthorized game servers, scams, malicious links, viruses, scripts, or promotional advertising;
4. Personal information that should not be public, including real names, contact details, passwords, verification codes, authorization tokens, or chat logs;
5. Content unrelated to the associated point or Arknights: Endfield, clearly incorrect information, repetitive spam, or material intended to mislead players.

## Moderation and interactions

1. New images and comments enter moderation, and edited comments are moderated again. The system may send relevant text or images to the **OpenAI Moderation API**. If automated moderation cannot determine a result, fails, or detects risk, the content proceeds to human review.
2. Approved content may be displayed publicly. Content under moderation or awaiting human review is normally visible only to its submitter and administrators.
3. Approved comments may be sent to Google Cloud Translation to pre-generate selected common-language translations or answer translation requests. Translation results may be cached.
4. Signed-in users may vote on or report public content when the product provides that capability. A report may place content into review but does not establish that the reported reason is valid.
5. We may hide, reject, flag, withdraw, or remove content that violates the rules and may restrict accounts for serious or repeated violations.

## Withdrawal, editing, and deletion

* Submitters may withdraw images or comments that remain in a withdrawable status;
* Comment authors may edit comments when their status allows, after which the edited version is reviewed again;
* Submitters may request removal of published content. Available operations for images and comments vary according to their current moderation or report status;
* If an operation cannot be completed in the product, use the contact information below to submit a request.

## Disclaimer

UGC is submitted independently by users. The project uses moderation and community-governance measures but does not guarantee that content is accurate, complete, lawful, or continuously available. Verify point images, comments, and route advice yourself and do not rely exclusively on UGC for decisions that could cause loss.

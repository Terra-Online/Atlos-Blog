title: User-Generated Content Statement
description: Rules for user-generated content in Open Endfield Map.
---

Signed-in users can add images and comments to existing points, reply, vote, report, and help maintain content. This statement supplements the [Terms of Use](./tos), [Privacy Policy](./privacy), and [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines).

## Scope, rights, and publication

UGC includes point images and guides, comments and edits, replies, votes, reports, shareable routes, annotations, and feedback. Completed-point status, theme, filters, and map view are not UGC. You retain rights in original content and warrant that you may submit it; submission grants OEM a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to store, review, translate, cache, convert, back up, display, and distribute it to operate and improve the service. Approved content, public author information, and interaction statistics can be seen by all visitors.

## Requirements and review

Do not submit content that infringes rights or law; contains sexual material, hate, harassment, threats, extremism, severe violence, cheats, scams, malicious links, advertising, or personal data and tokens; or is irrelevant, knowingly wrong, duplicate, or misleading. New and edited images and comments enter review. Text and images may be sent to the OpenAI Moderation API, with uncertain cases reviewed by people. Approved comments may be translated through Google Cloud Translation. Votes and reports initiate review but do not prove a violation. We may hide, reject, flag, withdraw, or remove content and restrict offending accounts.

## Withdrawal, deletion, and disclaimer

You may withdraw eligible content, edit comments subject to a new review, or request deletion. Removed records can be retained temporarily for security, disputes, backups, and legal duties. Users submit UGC independently; we do not guarantee its accuracy, legality, or continuing availability. Verify it against the game.

* **Reports and compliance**: [report@opendfieldmap.org](mailto:report@opendfieldmap.org)
